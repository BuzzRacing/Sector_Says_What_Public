Special logic

Designed around real broadcast failures

SSW is full of unglamorous guardrails because live racing data lies at exactly the wrong time. That is rude, but educational.

Single freeze per tickOne telemetry snapshot feeds the full engine tick so comparisons do not mix moments in time.
Qualifying grid authorityStarting grid comes from QualifyResultsInfo first, avoiding green-flag CarIdxPosition chaos.
Race-state gatesPre-green, white-flag, and post-checkered gates keep rolling commentary out of the wrong phase.
Position confirmationOvertakes use lap plus lap-distance sorting with confirmation windows and pit-road suppression.
DOTD as vote shareDriver of the Day is presented as percent of the positive vote, not a raw internal score.
Anti-fabrication stackSystem rules, prompt templates, and sanitizers block invented tyres, strategy, radio, emotions, and blame.
